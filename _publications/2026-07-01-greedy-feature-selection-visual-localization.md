An image frame carries far more point features than localization actually needs, and many of
them are redundant. Tracking all of them costs latency the robot cannot spare.

This paper gives two greedy algorithms for choosing the subset of features that is most
informative for localization over a prediction horizon. The emphasis is on the cost of the
selection step itself: both methods are built to reduce time and memory complexity, where
earlier approaches are demanding enough that the selection overhead eats into the benefit.
