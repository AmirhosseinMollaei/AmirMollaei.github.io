Risk in an uncertain map is not uniform. An unmapped volume behind a wall and an unmapped
volume in open floor space carry different consequences. This paper uses semantic labels in
the 3D Gaussian Splatting map so that the navigation policy reasons about what an uncertain
region is likely to be, not only about how uncertain it is.

Presented at the SeMaNa workshop, IROS 2026.
