**This paper was accepted as an invited session paper at CDC 2026.**

The most informative viewpoint is often the one closest to what the robot has not mapped yet,
so the perception objective and the safety constraint disagree. This paper makes that
disagreement explicit. Safety is enforced with control barrier functions defined over a 3D
Gaussian Splatting field, and the view-quality term enters the same quadratic program as a
constraint that can be relaxed when no safe informative motion is available.
