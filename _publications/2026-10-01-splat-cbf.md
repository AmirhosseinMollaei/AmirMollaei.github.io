**Status: under review at IEEE ICRA 2027.**

Next-best-view control where the safety certificate is built on the Gaussian-splat map itself.
The barrier condition is evaluated against the uncertainty the map carries, so the robot is
not relying on a thresholded occupancy grid to decide what is free.
