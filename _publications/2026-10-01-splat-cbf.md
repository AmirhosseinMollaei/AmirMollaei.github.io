**Status: under review at IEEE ICRA 2027. Submitted October 2026.**

The common route to a safety guarantee over a learned map is to threshold it into free and
occupied cells and plan against that. Thresholding throws away how confident the map was, and
the guarantee that comes out is a statement about the grid rather than about the scene.

Splat-CBF places the barrier condition on the Gaussian-splat representation itself, so the
safety check reads the uncertainty the splats carry. Next-best-view control then operates
inside that constraint: the robot can pursue informative viewpoints without leaving the set it
is still able to certify.
