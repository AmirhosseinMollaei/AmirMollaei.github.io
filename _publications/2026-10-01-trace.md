**Status: under review at IEEE ICRA 2027.**

Next-best-view selection over Gaussian-splat maps that are held across several agents, with
the selection done without requiring the agents to disclose their underlying maps to each
other.

A distributed next-best-view protocol that coordinates information acquisition across a team
while keeping each robot's own observations and Gaussian map parameters private to it.
